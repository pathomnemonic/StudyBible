# Draft: permission request to Catholic Answers (NOT SENT)

Catholic Answers' Terms of Service prohibit bots/scrapers and systematically retrieving data to build a database without written permission,
so this app does **not** crawl catholic.com. The app is ready to use their material the moment they agree: place rows in
`data/licensed/external-catholic-answers.jsonl` and run `npm run data:build` (see docs/INGESTION.md).

---
Subject: Request to link catholic.com articles to Bible verses in a free study app

Hello,

I'm building a free, non-commercial Catholic study Bible (public-domain Douay-Rheims/Vulgate/WEB-C, the Catechism by paragraph number, Haydock, the Catena Aurea and the Fathers).
Readers often want "what does Catholic Answers say about this passage?" Linking your articles to the verses they discuss would help many people find them, and every link would send readers to catholic.com.

What I'm asking: written permission to (a) maintain a mapping of your public article URLs and titles to the Bible passages they cite, and (b) show the title and a short
(≤ 200 character) description with a "Read on Catholic Answers" link. I would not store or display full article text. I'm happy to follow any attribution, rate, or removal requirements, or to use a feed or API if you have one.

Thank you for your work.
