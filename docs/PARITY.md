# How this app compares with other Catholic apps

An honest list, kept short. "Yes" means it works in the app now. "Partly" and "No" say what is missing and why.

| Area | Typical apps | Here |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Mass readings | Universalis, Laudate, USCCB | Yes (any day; Byzantine daily readings from Orthocal) |
| Mass follow-along | Laudate, Hallow | Partly: Mass mode gives every part of the Ordinary Form, posture, responses and today's readings; the Missal's own prayers (ICEL) are not printed. The 1962 Mass is in full in English with the propers of the day (Latin text not included). |
| Liturgy of the Hours | iBreviary, Universalis | Partly: traditional Roman psalter with seasons, and the feast and Matins lessons by date. Not the modern English Liturgy of the Hours (copyright). |
| Divine Liturgy and other rites | Few apps | Partly: Byzantine Divine Liturgy of St John Chrysostom (1914 English). The other Eastern rites, the Ordinariate and the Ambrosian and Mozarabic rites are listed with what is missing. |
| Bible translations | Laudate, Hallow | Partly: public-domain and open editions. NABRE, RSV-CE and other modern Catholic editions need a license and are not included. |
| Catechism | Laudate, Hallow | Yes: all paragraphs, search, Scripture links, Catechism in a Year |
| Papal writings | Laudate | Yes as a catalog of about 1,250 documents from Pius IX to Leo XIV linked to the Vatican's text. Full texts only where public domain. |
| Prayer book, Rosary, Stations, novenas, examination | All | Yes |
| Saints of the day, calendar | All | Yes |
| Audio: Bible in a Year, podcasts, recorded books | Hallow, Ascension | Yes, in one bottom player |
| Guided prayer with narration and music | Hallow, Pray As You Go | Partly: devotions and ambience; no commissioned narration (cost) |
| Study tools: commentary, Fathers, Summa, maps, words | Verbum, Logos, Catholic Study Bible | Yes for public-domain sources |
| Formation courses | FORMED, Ascension, Hallow | Yes: a K–12 and young adult catechism program with teacher handbook. Not video-led. |
| Parish tools, giving, bulletins, Mass-time finder | Parish apps, MassTimes | No: needs a server and parish data. Links to external finders only. |
| Community: groups, shared plans, live study | Hallow, Dynamic Catholic | Partly: share-by-link; no live groups (needs a server). |
| Canon law | Few | Link only (text is © Libreria Editrice Vaticana) |
| Offline use | Some | Yes |
| Cost to run | Subscriptions | None: no servers, no paid services |

## E-reader and podcast player parity (checked against Kindle, Apple Books, Goodreads, Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts)

Have: font, size, spacing, margins and themes; progress and time left; find in a section; dictionary-style word look-up; highlights in colors and notes by selecting text; a notebook of every highlight in a book; bookmarks; read aloud; a focus mode; swipe and keyboard turning; reading goals and a daily streak; shelves (want to read, reading, finished, did not finish), ratings, private reviews, collections, finished-this-year count; position synced across devices; share a quote; add your own EPUB, text, Markdown or HTML books. Podcasts: a library of 59 shows, search of the public directory, add by feed link, OPML import and export, follow, new episodes, resume where you stopped, queue with reorder, 0.5× to 3× speed remembered for each show, skip back and forward, skip intro, sleep timer (minutes or end of episode), lock-screen controls, show notes, share, mark played, your own audio files.

Not yet: downloading podcast episodes for offline listening (browsers cannot store most publishers' audio), podcast chapters, trim silence and voice boost, PDF reflow (PDFs must be converted to EPUB or text first), highlights shared with friends, page-turn animation, per-book typography.

## Teachers (checked against Google Classroom, Kahoot, Blooket, Gimkit, PowerSchool-style gradebooks)

Have: classes and rosters, attendance taken by joining a session, participation, a weighted gradebook with letter grades and due dates, rubrics, exams with a timer, printable tests with an answer key, report cards and family messages, student notes, a lesson planner, random picker, group maker, timer, live quiz games (quiz, last one standing, race, cooperative build), polls, CSV downloads. Sessions need no server of ours (a public relay carries sealed messages, or a local one in a classroom without internet).

Not yet: students seeing their own assignments and grades in the app, homework turned in through the app, parent accounts, a district-wide roster import (names can be pasted or loaded from a CSV), seating charts, a shared gradebook between co-teachers (records sync only to one account).
