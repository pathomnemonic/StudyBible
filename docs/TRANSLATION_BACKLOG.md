# Translation backlog

Texts that are in Latin (or another language) in the app's sources and are to be translated into English later. Nothing here is started unless it says so.

| Text | Source language | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glossa Ordinaria notes | Latin | Partly done: about 7,900 of 13,800 notes translated (AI-assisted). The rest are ready to hand to another AI. | See `docs/glossa-translation/README.md` for the hand-off kit and `scripts/glossa-import.py`. |
| Thomas Aquinas, Latin works (commentaries on the Gospels and Epistles and others that have no free English translation) | Latin | **Planned, not started.** | The Latin text of Aquinas is public domain, but the modern English translations (for example the 2010 Larcher/Weisheipl translation of the commentary on John, Catholic University of America Press) are under copyright, so they cannot be used. A fresh translation from the Latin would be needed. Choose a Latin edition with a clear licence first (check the Corpus Thomisticum and similar sources) and record it in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` before any work. |

Rules for every item: translations are labelled as machine- or AI-assisted until a human has checked them, the Latin original stays available beside the English, and nothing is added without a source whose rights are confirmed.
