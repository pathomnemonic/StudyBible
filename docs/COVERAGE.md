# How many verses have a reference

Counted on 2026-10-21 over the app's 35,809 verses (Clementine Vulgate numbering). A verse counts only when a note is anchored to it or to a short run of verses around it; notes about a whole chapter (about 80% of its verses or more) are not counted for any one verse. The script that produced these numbers is not kept in the repo; re-run the same counting after any data change.

| What | Verses |
|---|---|
| Anything anchored to the verse (cross-references, topics, dictionaries, people, Encyclopedia, commentary, Fathers, Catechism) | 33,202 (93%) |
| Nothing at all | 2,607 (7%) |
| Commentary, Fathers or Catechism (the Notes and Teaching tabs), Douay-Rheims open (includes Haydock) | 26,600 (74%) |
| The same, with a modern translation open (no Haydock; English notes only) | 19,714 (55%) |
| Verses with none of Haydock, Challoner, Catena, Lapide, English Glossa, a Father or the Catechism | 9,209 (26%), mostly Sirach, 1–2 Maccabees, Judith, Genesis, Numbers, Ezekiel, Jeremiah, Exodus, 1–2 Chronicles |
| New Testament with Notes or Catechism | 99% |
| Old Testament with Notes or Catechism (Douay open) | 71% |

By source (verses touched): cross-references 29,271; Nave 29,624; dictionaries 19,216; Haydock 18,922 (Douay only); people 16,477; Fathers and saints 15,890; Glossa 13,775 (7,886 translated into English); topics 11,087; Encyclopedia 10,800; Catechism 4,335; Catena 3,777; Lapide 2,784; Challoner 1,834.

The gaps are the deuterocanonical books (Haydock does not cover them), the Old Testament historical books, and the prophets.
