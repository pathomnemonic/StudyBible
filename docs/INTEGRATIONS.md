# Outside projects and data: what was adopted, what was not, and why

An outside review (14 November 2026) listed existing projects the app could build on. Each was checked against the code, the licence and what could be verified here. "Adopted" means shipped with a test; "not adopted" always has the reason.

| # | Resource | Decision | What was done / why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GregoBase scores (CC0) + Exsurge notation renderer (MIT) | **Adopted, in part** | Ten of the thirteen hymns on `/pray/hymns` show their melody in square-note notation (Veni Creator, Dies irae, Vexilla regis, Pange lingua, Tantum ergo, O salutaris, Adoro te, Regina caeli, Salve Regina, Te Deum). Scores are fetched by `scripts/chant/build.mjs` into `public/data/hymns/chant.json`; `tests/chant.test.ts` checks every score's sung words against the hymn's own Latin text (so a melody cannot sit under the wrong words), that each file has one header, and that Exsurge parses it; `e2e/chant.mjs` opens a score at 320, 390 and 1024 px. Exsurge is a separate 61 KB (gzip) chunk loaded only when a score is opened. **Not done:** Stabat Mater, Ave maris stella and Veni Sancte Spiritus (the app has no Latin text to check a score against); chants tied to the day's Mass (needs a reliable proper-by-proper mapping to the lectionary, which was not available to verify); recordings (GregoBase has none). |
| 2 | IIIF viewers (Universal Viewer, Mirador, OpenSeadragon) | **Not adopted** | The app already has a zoomable viewer for 13 open-access manuscripts. Adding IIIF means choosing manuscripts and checking each library's image rights and each folio's passage, none of which can be verified offline; Mirador 4 also needs React 19 (the app is on 18). Revisit with a named list of manuscripts and their rights statements. |
| 3 | STEPBible-Data (CC BY 4.0) | **Adopted, in part** | TAGNT's own notes on where the Greek editions differ in a way that changes the English: 1,630 notes on 27 New Testament books, shown per verse in the study panel's Words tab (`scripts/stepbible/build.mjs`, `public/data/lens/greek/<BOOK>.json`, `tests/greekEditions.test.ts`, `e2e/greek-editions.mjs`, attribution in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`). Famous places are asserted (Matthew 6:13, Luke 11:2, 1 John 5:7). **Not adopted:** TIPNR (its long descriptions are AI-generated by the project's own statement; the profile system holds validated, sourced entries), TVTMS (the app's own versification tables are already tested end to end), the lexicons (the original-language tools already have lexicon entries), and TAGOT (still in development). |
| 4 | Scaife Viewer and CSEL (Latin Fathers) | **Not adopted** | CSEL texts are machine-corrected and would need proofreading against scans; linking out to Scaife needs a verified CTS URN for each cited passage. Neither can be done without checking each work. |
| 5 | Open Christian Data | **Not adopted** | Licences are set text by text (the review itself says not to ingest it whole), and each work would need a rights check and a fact check. The library already has 171 works whose rights evidence is recorded one by one. |
| 6 | Pleiades (CC BY 3.0) | **Not adopted** | Overlaps the OpenBible geocoding already in the maps; 41,000 places, mostly outside the Bible, would add data weight (the data folder is at 278 of a 290 MB ceiling) for little biblical gain. |
| 7 | Museum APIs (Cleveland, Art Institute of Chicago) | **Not adopted** | The app already carries 600+ pictures (Doré engravings and museum paintings, 82 MB). More images are limited by the size ceiling, not by sources; and each new painting needs its subject tied to a passage by someone who has checked it. |
| 8a | LibriVox | **Already in the app** | Recordings are played by the existing player, with a daily refresh. |
| 8b | Podcasting 2.0 | **Adopted** | Chapters (from the feed's chapters file or the show notes) already worked. Added transcripts: `<podcast:transcript>` (WebVTT, SRT, JSON; never HTML) is offered in the full player as a fold, fetched only when opened, with lines that follow playback and seek when tapped (`tests/podcastTranscript.test.ts`, `e2e/transcript.mjs` with a controlled feed and audio). Feeds that publish no transcript, such as Bible in a Year, have none and none is generated. |
| 9 | Internet Archive BookReader | **Adopted as a link, not as code** | Eight library texts that came from Internet Archive scans offer "View the original scan", which opens the Archive's own embed (`archive.org/embed/<id>`) inside the page, only after the button is pressed (`e2e/scan.mjs` checks nothing is requested before). BookReader's own code is AGPL-3.0 and is not included. The privacy page lists the Archive. |
