Privacy Policy

PathoMnemonic — last updated November 22, 2026

PathoMnemonic makes a small family of study apps: Cathology (Catholic Bible study), Clinicle (daily medical mini-games), Dx Dash (medical board prep), and Language Dash (vocabulary practice). This policy covers all of them, on the web and in our mobile apps. The short version: our apps work without an account, we collect as little as we can, we never sell personal information, and we show no advertising.

Using the apps without an account

Every PathoMnemonic app can be used as a guest with no sign-in. In that case your progress, settings, and study data stay on your device, and nothing about your usage is sent to us. Some features that inherently need a server — such as Clinicle multiplayer rooms — create a temporary anonymous session so the feature can work; those sessions hold no name or email.

If you create an account

All of our apps share one sign-in, provided through Supabase. If you register, we store:

  • Your email address, and a securely hashed password if you use one (or your Google identity if you choose "Continue with Google").
  • A separate membership record inside each app you choose to join. Signing in to one app never enrolls you in another.
  • The data you create in each app — for example study progress, notes, highlights, game results, and scores — so it can sync across your devices.

Content from other publishers

Some content, such as videos, images and audio, is loaded directly from the publisher's own site. That site can see your IP address and the fact that you opened it. We don't send them anything else.

Payments

Paid upgrades ("Pro") are processed by Stripe on the web, or by Apple or Google in our mobile apps. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of what you purchased (plan, price, and status) so the app can unlock what you paid for and so we can help with refunds or problems. Tips are voluntary and unlock nothing; they are handled by the service the tip link opens (for example, Ko-fi).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not build profiles of you for anyone else.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors to run the service: Supabase (accounts and data storage), Stripe (web payments), Apple and Google (app-store payments and sign-in), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each receives only what it needs to do its job. Sign-in and account emails are sent from our own Gmail address.

Deleting your data

Every app has a delete option in its settings, labeled with that app's name (for example, "Delete my StudyBible account" in Cathology). Deleting an app's account removes that app's records for you and nothing else — your other apps are untouched. Most apps also offer a separate, clearly labeled option, "Also remove my sign-in," which deletes your shared sign-in itself; where an app does not offer that option, contact us at the address below and we will remove the sign-in for you. because that sign-in is shared, removing it also removes your data in every PathoMnemonic app. Both actions are permanent. You can also contact us at the address below and we will delete your data for you.

Optional Google Drive backup

Cathology offers an optional backup to your own Google Drive. If you use it, your browser talks to Google directly and the backup file lives in a hidden app folder of your own Drive, under your control. We never see it.

Children

Some of our lessons are written for younger learners. Lessons for younger learners are meant to be used by a parent, teacher or catechist. Children don't create accounts in our apps.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change here.

Contact
