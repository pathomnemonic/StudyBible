Privacy Policy

PathoMnemonic — last updated November 22, 2026

PathoMnemonic makes a small family of study apps: Cathology (Catholic Bible study), MedDaily (daily medical mini-games), Dx Dash (medical board prep), and Language Dash (vocabulary practice). This policy covers all of them, on the web and in our mobile apps. The short version: our apps work without an account, we collect as little as we can, we never sell personal information, and we show no advertising.

Using the apps without an account

Every PathoMnemonic app can be used as a guest with no sign-in. In that case your progress, settings, and study data stay on your device, and nothing about your usage is sent to us. Some features that inherently need a server — such as MedDaily multiplayer rooms — create a temporary anonymous session so the feature can work; those sessions hold no name or email.

If you create an account

All of our apps share one sign-in, provided through Supabase. If you register, we store:

  • Your email address, and a securely hashed password if you use one (or your Google identity if you choose "Continue with Google").
  • A separate membership record inside each app you choose to join. Signing in to one app never enrolls you in another.
  • The data you create in each app — for example study progress, notes, highlights, game results, and scores — so it can sync across your devices.

Payments

Paid upgrades ("Pro") and tips are processed by Stripe on the web, or by Apple or Google in our mobile apps. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of what you purchased (plan, price, and status) so the app can unlock what you paid for and so we can help with refunds or problems. Tips are voluntary, unlock nothing, and are handled the same way.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not build profiles of you for anyone else.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors to run the service: Supabase (accounts and data storage), Stripe (web payments), Apple and Google (app-store payments and sign-in), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each receives only what it needs to do its job. Sign-in and account emails are sent from our own Gmail address.

Deleting your data

Every app has a "delete my account data" option in its settings. Deleting an app's data removes that app's records for you and nothing else — your other apps are untouched. A separate, clearly labeled "remove my sign-in" option deletes your shared sign-in itself; because that sign-in is shared, removing it also removes your data in every PathoMnemonic app. Both actions are permanent. You can also contact us at the address below and we will delete your data for you.

Children

Our apps are study tools intended for a general audience of learners. They are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change here.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a deletion request, go to patho.mnemonic1@gmail.com.
