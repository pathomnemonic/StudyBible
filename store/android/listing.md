## Full description (4000)
Cathology is a free Catholic companion that works without an account and without a connection.

READ: the Bible in several translations, with the Church Fathers, the Catechism, cross-references and notes beside every verse.
PRAY: the daily Mass readings, the Rosary bead by bead, the Liturgy of the Hours, examen, litanies and a prayer list.
LEARN: a full catechism course for every grade, K to 12, and for adults, with teacher guides and slides. Saints, feasts, councils and doctrine by topic.
PLAY: daily Catholic word, quote and map games with shareable results.
YOURS: notes, highlights, journal and memorization stay on your device. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Works offline. Backups are a file you keep. Optional sync only if you set up your own server.
