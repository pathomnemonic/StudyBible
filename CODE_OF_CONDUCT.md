# Code of conduct

This project handles Scripture, Church teaching and people's private prayer. We ask contributors and reviewers to keep that in mind.

* **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree about the code, the data or the wording, not about the person. Catholics and non-Catholics, scholars and beginners, are all welcome here.
* **Respect the texts.** Do not alter Scripture or quoted sources to make a point. A change to a text, a quotation or a mapping needs a source.
* **Attribute everything.** Say where content comes from, who wrote it, and under what rights. Do not add material you do not have the right to redistribute. When in doubt, link to it instead of copying it.
* **Keep editorial and official apart.** App-written notes, summaries and questions must not be presented as Church teaching, and a source's authority level must not be inflated.
* **Do not claim more than the data shows.** A match "by the calendar" or "by title" is labelled that way. Say what has and has not been verified.
* **Treat corrections as gifts.** A report that something is misattributed or wrongly mapped is the most useful thing someone can send. Thank them, fix it or explain, and record it.
* **Protect private data.** Never paste real notes, journal entries, session cookies or passwords into an issue or pull request. See SECURITY.md for reporting vulnerabilities privately.

Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions that break these expectations.
